About CGGAP

Empowering voices.
Cultivating peace.

At the Center for Good Governance and Peace, we bridge the gap between community voices and institutional power. We work directly on the ground to turn political transitions into collaborative progress, equipping grassroots advocates, local leaders, and youth with the practical tools needed to track budgets and demand accountability. We foster cross-continental alliances, host vital multi-stakeholder dialogues and are actively shaping transparent systems that secure a more equitable, peaceful, and honest future for everyone.
